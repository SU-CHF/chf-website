This note extracts the clauses that govern CHF documents. The manual governs; where the two
disagree, the manual wins. Page numbers are the manual's own, printed in its footers.

## Logo

**Orientations (4.1.3, p22).** SU names three: *portrait*, *horizontal*, *circular*.

> "The portrait option works well when space is an issue."
> "The horizontal logo is the preferred logo for digital media and mainly used in landscape formats
> where space and formatting are wide, or where there are height restrictions."
> "The circular option is reserved for rare cases and specified sub-brands only."

The manual ties orientation to available space. It gives **no cover-specific rule**.
The CHF artwork files are named *horizontal*, *stacked*, and *vertical* — "stacked" is SU's portrait.

**Clear space (4.1.4, p23).**

> "At a minimum, the space should always be twice the height of the capital 'S' of the word
> 'Stellenbosch'. In exceptional cases, half of this width is permitted."
> "The clear space surrounding the brand signature is exactly the same as the rule established for the logo."

**Minimum sizes (4.1.4, p23–24).**

| Element | Print | Digital |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait logo | 15 mm height | 60 px height |
| Horizontal logo | 25 mm width | 95 px width |
| Portrait brand signature | 30 mm height | 85 px height |
| Horizontal brand signature | 50 mm width | 142 px width |
| Circular logo | 25 mm | — |

**Colourways (4.1.5, p25).** Only three applications are permitted:

> "1. Brilliant Gold and white on a Confident Maroon background
> 2. Brilliant Gold and Confident Maroon on a white background
> 3. Black-and-white. This option is only to be used if the entire artwork is being produced in black-and-white."

On a **light** image background use gold-and-maroon; on a **dark** image background use white,
"ensure that there is enough contrast and that the logo doesn't 'disappear' into the background image."

**Never (4.1.6, p26).** Scale elements separately · add gradients or effects · separate elements ·
change colour · fade or tint · move or scale elements · use the 'S'-stream symbol alone · drop shadow ·
rotate · outline · stretch or skew · use on any background colour other than those specified.

**Placement (4.1.7, p27).**

> "Our logo takes the lead in our communication, starting from the left. The preferred and
> recommended placement position is top left or bottom left."

**File formats (4.1.3, p22).** Professionally designed communications: EPS or SVG.
Microsoft Office: JPG, PNG or SVG. Digital: JPG, PNG or SVG. Only supplied artwork may be used.

## Colour

### Primary (4.2.1, p30)

| | Confident Maroon | Brilliant Gold |
|---|---|---|
| Pantone | 209 C | 465 C |
| Metallic Pantone | — | 871 C ⚠ |
| CMYK | 15 / 96 / 31 / 55 | **26 / 40 / 73 / 3** |
| RGB | 97 / 34 / 59 | 202 / 162 / 88 |
| Hex | #61223B | #CAA258 |
| Vinyl | Dark Burgundy 863 | Gold Metallic 836 |

> ⚠ **The manual contradicts itself.** The metallic gold is **872 C** on p21 (4.1.2, logo colour values)
> and **871 C** on p30 (4.2.1, primary colours). Both are real Pantone metallic golds. CHF cites 871 C,
> following the colour specification. Confirm with branding@sun.ac.za before any metallic print run.

**Tints.** "We recommend only tints of 10%, 20%, 40% and 60% in the primary colour palette",
and only "on inside spreads in document or presentation layouts when creating tables and infographics
with large amounts of information." 10% Confident Maroon on white ≈ `#EFE9EB`.

### Neutrals (4.3.2, p36) — the body-copy colours

| | Black | Steel Grey |
|---|---|---|
| Pantone | Process Black | 445 C |
| CMYK | 0 / 0 / 0 / 100 | 0 / 0 / 0 / 80 |
| RGB | 44 / 42 / 41 | 77 / 83 / 86 |
| Hex | #2C2A29 | #4D5356 |

"80%–100% black should be used for body text."

### Secondary (4.2.2, p31)

Seabreeze Green `#82CCAE` · Wine Red `#A60A3D` · Fiery Orange `#DC4405` · Soil `#643335`.
"Always use Confident Maroon or white as the dominant colour." Tints of 20% and 40% only.

### Faculty (4.2.3, p32)

Economic and Management Sciences — Pantone 319 C, CMYK 60/0/25/0, RGB 44/204/211, `#2CCCD3`.
"Faculty colours should never overpower our primary colours."

### Ratios (4.2.4, p33)

- **Primary ratio** — 70% Confident Maroon · 20% Brilliant Gold · 10% secondary colours.
  For "professional administrative support services (PASS) divisions and sub-brands".
- **Faculty ratio** — 60% Confident Maroon · 10% Brilliant Gold · 30% faculty colour.
  For "faculty-specific communication material".

> "These charts should not be interpreted literally, as they are meant only as a visual
> representation of the relative importance of the different colours."

Which applies to a research centre inside a department inside a faculty is genuinely ambiguous.
The CHF guidelines cite both and leave the choice to the author.

## Typography (4.3, p35–38)

- Primary typeface: **SU Raleway**. "Please make sure you're using SU Raleway, where the numerals
  align on the baseline" — i.e. *not* Raleway from Google Fonts.
- Weights: Bold and above for headlines; SemiBold and below for subheadings; Medium and below for body copy.
- **Alternate system font: Trebuchet** (4.3.3) — "freely available on all computers". Not Arial.
- "Please ensure that Ligatures are disabled when typesetting SU Raleway."
- Headings and subheadings in Confident Maroon; body copy 80–100% black.
- Body copy shown at 11 pt.
- **Leading** "should be the same or 2 points higher than your point size, especially when the copy is small."
- Reversing type out of a colour "you may need to use a heavier weight to ensure legibility."
- "The typeface can only be used in its original form. You should never manipulate, distort or replace it."

## What changed in the CHF guidelines (v1.1 → v1.2)

Two corrections after a website build surfaced them. Both concern the **use of maroon**, and
neither had any basis in the SU manual.

| # | v1.1 said | SU says | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | “Restraint — maroon and gold are accents on a white page, not a background wash”; ratios footnote described “a white page carrying maroon headings, structure, and logo” | Colourway 1 (4.1.5) is gold-and-white **on a Confident Maroon background**; the primary ratio (4.2.4) is **70% Confident Maroon**; secondary-colour rule (4.2.2): “Always use Confident Maroon or white as the dominant colour.” Maroon headers/footers/title fields are on-brand, not prohibited. | Fixed — principle renamed “Maroon leads”; white-dominant framing removed |
| 17 | No web/digital guidance; document scoped to “letters, reports, and slides” | SU’s own digital properties (su.ac.za, resep.sun.ac.za) use maroon headers and footers | Fixed — added §7 Web & digital (maroon header/footer, gold-and-white logo, screen minimums, colourways) |

The numeric ratio table was already correct in v1.1; the residual prose in §1 and §3 that contradicted
it was the problem. v1.2 removes it.

## Artwork corrections made in this folder

`SU official logo - colour formats/RGB/Gold and White_RGB/` contained
`CHF_logo_maroon_gold_horizontal_RGB.svg` and `.png`, which actually hold the **white-and-gold**
horizontal artwork (fills `#fff` and `#caa258`). Both were renamed to
`CHF_logo_white_gold_horizontal_RGB.*`. Every other file in that tree was audited — colour operators
inside each PDF, SVG, PNG, and JPEG were checked against its filename — and the rest are named correctly.
