# Founding announcements, 2025

These three items ran as a News section on the Centre's landing page from March
2025 until the site was reorganised in August 2026. They are the Centre's
founding record, kept here so the text is not lost when the section came off.

Worth reusing in the first Directors' Report, or as a first Insights post on the
Centre's opening year.

---

**March 2025.** We welcomed [Dr Matthew Olckers](https://matthewolckers.com/) as
a senior researcher and founding member of our group. Matthew has worked on a
range of household finance topics, including retirement saving and financial
literacy.

**February 2025.** [Dr Allan Davids](https://allandav.github.io/), a senior
lecturer at the University of Cape Town, joined our group as an affiliated
researcher and founding member. Allan's research focuses on housing and credit.

**January 2025.** [Dr Jesse Naidoo](https://jnaidoo.rbind.io/) joined
Stellenbosch University as the founding director of our research centre.
[Here is an interview](https://www.ekon.sun.ac.za/blog/2025-1-17-jesse-naidoo-heads-new-centre)
with Jesse describing his vision for the Centre.
